# Fixed-regimen continuation decision

Both original-parent resumes completed without retry: 2,101,842 new physical
executions, 1,893,614 transition hands, and 3,374,686 replay rows. Training wall
time was 4096.555 seconds (513.1 physical executions/second combined). Controller
wall time including evaluation and checks was 6305.813 seconds.

All four fixed evaluations completed 131,072 executions. Independent terminal
review passed: 16,384 unique evaluation decks, zero overlap with 1,165,314 prior
inventoried rows, preserved checkpoint/source hashes, identical endpoint outcomes
across the two baseline comparisons, retained initial Adam/replay/reference state,
continuous metric/assignment suffixes, distinct managed attempt namespaces.
This is statistical training continuation, not bitwise worker equivalence or proof
of unique training deals. Evaluation panels share ancestry; they are not evidence
of lifetime-unseen opponent generalization. Final Slumbot qualification hands: zero.

Endpoint minus extension start, pooled bb/100:
- Seed1: -9.8206, conditional unadjusted 95% CI [-22.5773, 2.9362].
- Seed3: -6.0381, CI [-18.6100, 6.5338].

Endpoint minus earlier fixed-regimen root:
- Seed1: +0.3878, CI [-11.3000, 12.0756].
- Seed3: -6.1113, CI [-20.2376, 8.0149].

Extension-start preservation panel means are -7.5562/-2.1396 and transfer means
-12.0850/-9.9365 for seeds1/3; all panel CIs span zero. Both overall seat means
are negative in both seeds. Transfer versus earlier root is also negative in both
seeds. No comparison satisfies the preregistered directional breadth condition.
The severe-collapse condition is not met: no comparison has three anchors with
upper CI below -25. Isolated unadjusted anchor/seat findings are exploratory, not
multiple-testing-controlled discoveries. Better results against Standard10 alone
do not establish preservation or broader poker strength.

Decision: no automatic 4M, no final-policy promotion, no new Slumbot cohort for
these endpoints. This bounded unchanged extension supplies no replicated positive
learning direction. It does not prove equivalence, inevitable collapse, or that
longer training could never work. Compute is affordable but the observed direction
does not justify further unchanged scale under the registered allocation rule.

Next: reassess the learning objective and effective update signal using the retained
evidence, then preregister one major learning-method control. Do not open another
pool/replay/ensemble parameter sweep or rerun completed gradient diagnostics.
Any new method must target general strength and earn scale through multi-seed,
both-seat, heterogeneous-opponent learning curves and external calibration.
