## Scientific decision

Reject this unchanged frozen checkpoint for a final100k allocation. It does not meet the goal, and repeating/extending it until a lucky positive result would not be a valid blind test. No goal completion or promotion is claimed.

The static family's unaffected internal2M-to-4M slope was near zero and uncertain; this external result supplies no affirmative evidence that the extra training improved general strength. Low drift from Standard10 does not establish good external transfer. Conversely, this one external seed and an inconclusive baseline difference do not prove that long-horizon learning cannot work, nor identify a causal leaking action, critic fault or universal failure of the algorithm family. Do not reverse losing actions, train on Slumbot action labels or restart the old single-imitation-teacher route.

The next main research question is whether a reference held through substantial inner training phases can release the permanent fixed-reference constraint more effectively than unchanged static training. Select one matched phase-held-reference control, not a collection of temperature/gradient/action patches. The prior interval1 moving-reference result rejects that specific rapid-refresh intervention, not all phased regularized self-play. Retain the current static recipe as the one main comparator, preserve original Seed1 weights/optimizer/replay/league state, and use the now-qualified multi8 managed runtime. Fix phase schedule, geometric budgets, checkpoint identities, freshness semantics and evaluation/stop rules prospectively before training; require independent-seed replication before a population-level gain claim. This is a local matched method control, not a faithful paper-scale NashPG reproduction.
