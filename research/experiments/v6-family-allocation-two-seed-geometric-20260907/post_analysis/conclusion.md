## Report defect retained, not erased

The frozen summarizer hardcodes the four preservation anchor names. Its adaptation
by-anchor entries therefore have zero samples; positive-anchor counts and the
adaptation broad-collapse check based on those entries are invalid. Original
stage analyses are preserved. post_terminal_review.json independently recomputes
actual anchor buckets from raw paired rewards and is the corrected evidence.
Pooled and seat statistics match the originals. Corrected severe-collapse gates
are false for both panels, seeds and stages, so the observed continuation decision
does not change. This does not excuse the defect or validate other historical
uses of the helper; those require a bounded impact review before reuse.

## Resource decision

Close this completed, evidence-corrected trial; do not promote stratification,
launch final Slumbot qualification, or keep tuning allocation fractions.
First qualify an isolated fail-closed arbitrary-anchor reporting helper and
bound the historical impact of this discovered defect without new poker hands.
Then make the next main training allocation using replicated own-root curves,
heterogeneous opponent breadth and external calibration, rather than another
allocation-only tweak. Current data do not establish a stable winning policy.
