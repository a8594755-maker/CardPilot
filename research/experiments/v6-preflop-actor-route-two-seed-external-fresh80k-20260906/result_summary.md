## Interpretation and next allocation

Classification: execution/evidence valid with disclosed preparation/naming
deviations; cross-seed transfer gain unconfirmed; all current external endpoints
negative. This is not evidence for a winning model, a robust intervention benefit,
or paper-scale allocation. It is also not a mechanism-based disproof of longer-run
learned-weight training: each actor-route branch has only about1M post-split
physical hands, and the between-arm/parent contrasts remain imprecise.

Choose ONE bounded continuation experiment, not another small algorithmic
variant: retain both seeds, connected main family and detached control, and
continue unchanged optimizer/replay/opponent/gradient-route configuration from
the existing four1M endpoints to2,097,152 post-split physical hands per branch.
Use exact retained state with new managed attempt namespaces, account only new
actual executions, and preserve all earlier offsets and unknown interrupted tail.
This is a training-scale uncertainty experiment, NOT promotion of connected
mode or permission to proceed beyond2M automatically.

To reduce proxy-only cost, use one fixed terminal internal breadth check of
512new common-deck pairs per anchor over the same four anchors and both seats,
instead of another dense131k/262k internal confirmation. Retain the original
common parent for cross-arm comparability; clearly label uncertainty and reuse
earlier curve evidence without rerunning its old hands. Internal screening is
a broad-regression diagnostic, not a requirement for a positive pooled CI.
Then require separately preregistered fixed external calibration of ALL four2M
endpoints before deciding on any next geometric scale. Do not cherry-pick

If additional scale repeats proxy/external contradiction or broad regression,

changes. It does not assert that more hands must help. The next experiment needs
