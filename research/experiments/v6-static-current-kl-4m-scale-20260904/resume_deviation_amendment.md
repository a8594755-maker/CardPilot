# Outcome-blind amendment: Seed2 deterministic deal-prefix reuse

Recorded 2026-09-04 before any 4M endpoint evaluation is started. Original
preregistration, seeds, checkpoints, hand targets, sessions and raw evidence
remain unchanged. This amendment changes interpretation, not the trained policy.

## Established operational deviation

Both Seed2 recovery processes reused the original stage's worker seeds and
fixed deal start. Frozen-prefix evidence in the resume integrity experiment
establishes 441,445 repeated exposures at that observation boundary. The final
count will be reconstructed after Seed2 is frozen. Actual completed hand counts
remain executions, not unique deals. Uncheckpointed unknown tails stay excluded
from exact overlap claims. We do not infer duplicated actions or trajectories.

## Evaluation remains useful but is not a clean three-seed scale confirmation

Preserve the original evaluation endpoints, four anchors, both seats, deck seeds,
sample budgets, frozen execution modes and drift checks. Report every seed,
including Seed2. Do not select endpoints or seeds using their poker outcomes.

In addition to the original all-three descriptive aggregate, report the predeclared
unaffected-by-this-incident subset (Seed1 and Seed3) separately, explicitly as two
seeds and not a substitute three-seed confirmation. Their inclusion is based on
the process/deal evidence before their evaluation outcomes, not favorable scores.
These labels do not certify every older lineage boundary as free of other issues.

Do not run a fourth seed or rerun the already-trained prefix just to replace an
unfavorable result. Any clean continuation control must be justified and separately
registered after the available evidence changes the next allocation decision.

## Mechanical audit and promotion

Retain mechanical audit failures as data. Do not add fake passing recovery
preflights, change historical hashes or broadly bypass a failed audit. Verify
checkpoint/optimizer/replay/model contract and raw evidence independently from
the freshness/scale claim. If unrelated integrity failures appear, investigate
before evaluation. Known training-deal reuse alone does not prevent measuring a
frozen policy on genuinely fresh evaluation decks.

The original 8M promotion gate cannot automatically authorize further unchanged
scale because its clean three-seed premise failed. Compute the original gate
descriptively, disclose this deviation, and make a new explicit resource decision
after all results are available. This is not a claim that the method failed or that
the policies cannot improve with larger training.
