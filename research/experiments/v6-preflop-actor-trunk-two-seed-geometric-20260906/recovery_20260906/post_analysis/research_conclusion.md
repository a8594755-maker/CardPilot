# Fixed actor-route trial: research conclusion

The fixed experiment and its recovery are complete. This is not completion of the
Slumbot goal. The terminal review and learning-curve review passed, with no new
poker hands generated by either review. The original interrupted attempt, its
unknown exit cause/time and its unknown uncheckpointed worker tail remain intact.

## What the experiment establishes

Both seeds completed detached and connected continuations at approximately
262,144 and 1,048,576 additional physical hands per arm. The connected intervention
routes preflop PPO, entropy and current-to-reference KL gradients into the shared
representation. The direct critic-to-trunk gradient stays detached in both arms.
All 86 tensors remain trainable, with restored Adam LR approximately 1e-4. The
comparison is an equal-environment-dose training intervention, not an equal-Adam-
step experiment and not proof that only shared-body tensors differ after PPO.

The independent review reconstructed 262,144 internal executions from 32,768
unique new common decks across the two stages. Parent payouts and decks match
across arms within seed/stage; stages and seeds are disjoint, with no overlap in
706,562 inventoried prior common-deck rows. This scope is not every historical
poker corpus. Standard10 is also a training reference, so the four anchors must
not all be described as unseen opponents.

## Strength evidence

Values are bb/100; brackets are nominal conditional paired-deck 95% intervals.
They quantify evaluation-deck uncertainty, not variation across training seeds,
multiple-comparison-adjusted confirmation, or external poker strength.

Connected-minus-detached has positive point estimates in both seats and three of
four anchors in each seed. CFR4 is the negative anchor in both seeds (-22.3857
and -8.1875); none of these arm-contrast anchor/seat intervals excludes zero.
Standard10 arm contrasts are +19.7876 and +6.7871, but connected-minus-parent
Standard10 values are -12.2070 and +3.8086, also inconclusive. Lower KL therefore
does not establish preservation of poker capability.

From the 262k to the 1M endpoint, connected-minus-original-parent changes are
-6.6499 [-31.7807, 18.4809] for Seed1 and +2.9484 [-23.6271, 29.5238] for Seed3.
The changes in connected-minus-detached advantage are +11.0845
[-16.2784, 38.4473] and -1.8025 [-25.3293, 21.7243]. These are disjoint-stage
conditional comparisons, not trajectories measured on identical evaluation decks.
They do not demonstrate replicated positive learning slopes. Neither stage met
the preregistered severe broad-collapse gate.

The favorable Seed3 absolute interval is not a learned improvement over its
parent, and cannot be cherry-picked as a new best model. Seed1's deterioration
and the CFR4 weakness must remain visible. Classification: mechanically valid;
modest, unconfirmed relative-benefit signal; insufficient evidence for a stronger
general policy. This does not rule out longer-run learning at larger scale.

## Learning and resource evidence

Stage2 Adam increments were 614 detached / 680 connected for Seed1 and 620 / 652
for Seed3, across all 86 existing states. Mean current-to-reference KL was
0.070606 / 0.008646 and 0.079678 / 0.011322, respectively. Connected mode had
fewer KL early-stop iterations, so its realized update dose differs as well as
its gradient routing. Finite updates, lower KL and entropy are mechanism/health
evidence, not poker-strength results or causal attribution of the score changes.

The experiment retained 4,203,389 evidenced new physical training hands:
3,716,560 transition-generating hands, 484,585 no-decision hands and 2,244
accounted residual worker-tail hands. The 6,494,926 replay rows are not new
environment hands. Final per-branch added physical counts are 1,048,836
(Seed1 detached), 1,052,940 (Seed1 connected), 1,050,633 (Seed3 detached), and
1,050,980 (Seed3 connected). The largest single lineage counter is 11,551,174;
earlier head-only training is not relabeled as full-representation or connected
preflop training. Slumbot hands and final-qualification hands in this record are 0.

All 16 normally completed jobs and 112 observed child identities are terminal.
Nine distinct attempt namespaces include the preserved partial attempt and its
remainder. The partial's 8,987 physical hands are counted once. Restored model,
optimizer, replay, pool, counters and main RNG passed actual-state checks;
worker continuation remains statistical, not bitwise. Additional interrupted
in-flight worker hands are unknown, not zero; total actual executions are at
least the evidenced count.

Completed training subprocess wall time is 16,760.9547 s. Including the
interrupted job's observation-bound duration yields [16,952.2444, 17,394.7087] s
of training time and retained throughput [241.6476, 247.9547] physical hands/s.
These are observation bounds, not confidence intervals or isolated hardware
benchmarks. Completed evaluation jobs cost 7,279.0226 s (121.3170 min): stage1
2,529.7862 s and stage2 4,749.2364 s. Shared resource observations do not establish
why throughput varied. Logger wall time additionally includes preparation,
recovery, analysis and idle overhead; it is not interchangeable with training cost.

## Next compute allocation

Do not promote a policy or automatically allocate paper-scale training. Retain
this connected family and its detached control; do not discard the family merely
because this short curve lacks a proven gain. The next useful decision is whether
the modest same-direction internal contrast transfers externally, given the
project's earlier internal/external ranking reversals and the inconclusive
same-seed parent comparisons here.

Preregister one fixed-endpoint external DEVELOPMENT calibration using all four
final policies, 20,000 fresh Slumbot hands per policy (80,000 total), balanced
cohorts and independent session evidence. Preserve the exact weights, runtime,
observation/action contract and execution mode. Do not select only Seed3 or the
best stage, pool policies as one policy, train on Slumbot labels, or call these
hands final qualification. Reuse qualified evaluation infrastructure after
input/contract qualification rather than introducing another algorithmic arm.

This allocation is a calibration and gross-regression check, not a claim that
20k per policy will resolve a true 4-9 bb/100 difference. Its uncertainty and
cross-seed/seat behavior, together with this curve and actual cost, will decide
whether the next geometric training step is justified. No requirement to win at
1M is introduced. A much larger repeated internal confirmation of this small
contrast would be costly and would still not resolve proxy transfer. No new
training, external hands or final 100k test is authorized by merely closing this
record; the next experiment needs its own prospective registration and checks.
