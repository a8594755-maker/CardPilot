## Interpretation and allocation

The within-seed LR contrast directions agree with the preceding internal final
comparison (+8.6078 for Seed1 and -10.9086 for Seed3). This is limited directional
calibration, not validation of every internal proxy. Lower learning rate and lower
KL did not produce a reproducible strength gain. The earlier Seed3 half-rate
stage1 improvement reversed during stage2 internally, and the external final
point estimate also disfavors half rate. None of this proves a causal universal
failure of full-representation learning or that approximately 2.1M full-scope
hands per lineage is sufficient training.

Do not allocate a final blind test, select a lucky seed/stage, automatically add
millions to the same LR comparison, or discard retained models. Retire the half-LR
control from the next allocation; keep both original-LR final lineages as matched
parents, including the worse Seed1. One general, single-change control has higher
information value than more LR tuning: qualify an opt-in preflop actor-to-shared-
representation gradient route while retaining critic_v2's detached value route.
The existing preflop detach originated as teacher-preservation isolation; current
RL uses a learned preflop head and no preflop teacher loss. Existing synthetic
gradient evidence establishes missing connectivity, not the cause of these losses.

Next: inspect prior evidence and current trainer, implement the opt-in route with
default behavior unchanged, then verify forward parity, per-street actor and
critic gradients, exact optimizer/replay/counter restoration and explicit resume
contract provenance on both retained original-LR parents. Only after qualification
preregister a two-seed connected-versus-detached geometric learned-weight trial.
Keep opponents, physical rules, inference contract and critic routing matched;
do not add Slumbot labels, action rules, new architecture heads or an extra LR arm.
No production training or next experimental outcome is claimed by this decision.
