# Post-replay allocation decision

Bounded synthesis of four completed independently audited experiments: 48
own-root cells (stage, seed, panel, arm), with original panel identities retained.
This does not repeat raw audits and does not combine dependent results into a
meta-analytic confidence interval.

No final arm/panel has positive lower confidence bounds in both seeds. Only the
capacity5 control in the expanded-family trial has positive final point estimates
in both seeds: +0.278 [-12.497,13.053] and +8.011 [-4.924,20.947] bb/100; Seed1
does not improve in both seats. This is weak evidence, not evidence of failure.
The family-allocation corrected report is used, not its invalid old adaptation
anchor summaries. The fresh-only early positive contrasts did not replicate at
the final dose. Internal opponents share ancestry; none of this is final Slumbot
qualification or universal generalization evidence.

Measured subprocess throughput spans 355-542 physical executions/second, with
resource contention and different update work confounded. At the latest aggregate
505/s, 2.7B aggregate executions would take approximately 62 continuous days before
evaluation, outages or review, not 2.7B per seed. Large scale is feasible in time
but is not justified merely by completed execution counts. Changing configurations
between retained parents means their cumulative counters are not one unchanged
algorithm's long-run learning curve. No cross-stage paired difference is inferred
from summary CIs on different evaluation decks.

Decision: preserve all endpoints, do not promote fresh-only, and do not launch
another replay/pool-allocation sweep or automatic 4M/external qualification.
Next bounded hypothesis is temporal policy stability: determine whether retained
outcome-independently selected checkpoint boundaries support a frozen temporal
policy aggregate versus the final iterate. This is not an assertion that cycling
exists or averaging improves poker. First qualify complete observation/action
compatibility, legal normalized probabilities and immutable source identities;
do not average optimizer states or silently average incompatible network tensors.
Use both existing seed lineages and dose-selected boundaries, never winning-hand
labels or best-scoring checkpoint selection. A subsequent preregistered matched
multi-anchor evaluation must include final iterate and own root. If qualification
cannot establish an interpretable fixed contract, do not run the evaluation.
Any aggregate strength result would still need new external calibration and does
not establish that an altered training algorithm learns faster.

This review used zero new poker hands and changed no existing checkpoints.
