# Preregistration: static-current-KL high-pot critic calibration

## Question

Does the retained static-current-KL method develop a replicated, high-pot-specific
critic-calibration defect between its independently trained 262k and 1M endpoints?
This is a zero-Slumbot, read-only diagnostic motivated by the separately confirmed
external `final_pot_bucket=ge30` loss concentration. It does not use Slumbot actions,
rewards, or labels as targets.

## Frozen inputs and execution contract

- Three seed-matched control/treatment pairs: the final checkpoints from
  `v6-static-current-kl-262k-scale-20260903` and
  `v6-static-current-kl-1m-scale-20260903`.
- Four unchanged general anchors: Standard10, CFR4, legacy iter16, and legacy
  mixed65k.
- Greedy physical-v6 play through the legacy-v4 observation bridge, both candidate
  seats, 512 fresh common-deck pairs per anchor and seed.
- Evaluation seeds are `20263121..20263123`, with the existing anchor stride
  `1,000,003`. Generated decks must be unique in this experiment and absent from
  all raw 262k, 262k-confirmation, and 1M static-current-KL evaluations.
- The checkpoint SHA256 values are captured before model loading and verified again
  after evaluation.

This produces 12,288 on-policy hands per checkpoint (24,576 total). No model is
updated. The critic contract must be `critic_v2` with output scale 200bb.

## Sampling unit and targets

For each candidate hand, retain at most the first candidate decision in each
predeclared current-pot bucket: `<10bb`, `[10bb,30bb)`, and `>=30bb`. This avoids
treating repeated decisions from one hand as independent within a bucket. For a
retained decision, the realized target is the final candidate payoff discounted by
`gamma=0.999` once per remaining candidate decision, matching the critic's decision
sequence Bellman convention. Residual is `target_bb - prediction_bb`.

Descriptive per-policy/per-seed/per-bucket outputs are count, bias and its normal
95% CI, MAE, RMSE, OLS calibration intercept/slope, and prediction/target means.
Inference is strictly on-policy: a checkpoint is scored only on states generated by
its own frozen actor.

## Primary matched comparisons

The primary unit is `(seed, anchor, deck-pair, candidate-seat)`. High-pot comparisons
include only identities for which both the 262k and 1M actor reached `>=30bb` and
therefore have one eligible critic observation. For each identity compute 1M minus
262k squared-error and absolute-error deltas. Normal 95% CIs are computed over these
independent hand identities and also separately by seed.

The high-pot specificity comparison is fixed before seeing results. For identities
where both policies have both `<10bb` and `>=30bb` observations, compute:

`(1M high-pot squared error - 262k high-pot squared error) -
 (1M low-pot squared error - 262k low-pot squared error)`.

## Decision gate

Admit a general high-pot value-calibration/resampling intervention only if all hold:

1. Every seed has at least 100 common-reach high-pot identities.
2. High-pot squared-error delta is positive in at least two of three seeds.
3. The pooled high-pot squared-error-delta 95% CI lower bound is above zero.
4. The pooled high-minus-low difference-in-differences 95% CI lower bound is above
   zero and its point estimate is positive in at least two seeds.
5. At 1M, high-pot mean residual has a same-sign 95% CI excluding zero in at least
   two seeds.

If the gate fails, the earlier Slumbot class remains a descriptive external risk
localization, not evidence for action reversal, high-pot oversampling, critic reset,
or solver-label imitation. If it passes, the next experiment may train a general
state-coverage/value-calibration intervention, still without Slumbot labels, and
must retain untouched future external cohorts for blind evaluation.
