## Implication — corrects the queue's E3 framing
The BC anchor IS heuristic_v3 to within 0.9% total variation. Therefore:
- **"E3 = train BC better / longer on the same teacher" is DEAD.** BC is already saturated
  (L1-TV 0.009); more BC epochs/data on `teacher_v3_5M` cannot move the -45 ceiling. The ceiling
  is the **heuristic_v3 strategy's** ceiling, not a cloning gap.
- **E3 only helps if the TEACHER gets stronger.** But heuristic v1/v2/v3/v3.1 are frozen
  (doctrine). A genuinely stronger hand-written teacher (a hypothetical heuristic v4) is *real new
  engineering*, not a cheap re-BC. So the cycle-6 label "E3 cheap+ready, lifts -45" was optimistic:
  the *re-BC* is cheap, but it lifts nothing without a new teacher, and a new hand-built teacher is
  not cheap.
- **The only "stronger teacher" we already possess is the CFR policy (vnet-v10).** Relabeling /
  blending the teacher data with CFR targets and re-BC = **E2-adjacent** (CFR→policy distillation).
  Caveat unchanged: CFR is the SRP/3bet-50bb abstraction, so it cannot relabel full-200bb states it
  never solved (coverage gap) — but where it DOES cover, it is a strictly stronger label than v3.
